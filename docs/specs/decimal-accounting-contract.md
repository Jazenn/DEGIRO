# Decimal accounting read model

`calculate_portfolio(...).to_read_model()` is the additive public contract for new hosted consumers. It includes ledger/settings/calculation versions, cutoff, observed-price provenance and `ledger-v1`. All money and quantities serialize as decimal strings; unavailable figures serialize as null. Display consumers round explicitly, and no execution precision is implied. The older DataFrame and float fields remain a legacy dashboard adapter (`canonical-v1`); they are excluded from this new read model and must not supply new valuations.

`ledger.positions` retains completed Assets as well as held Assets. It exposes broker identity, explicitly verified symbol/kind, quantity, retained FIFO lots with transaction identity/acquisition date and fee-inclusive recorded cost, current observed market value, held result and cumulative realized result. `ledger.charges` retains actual dates, categories and allocation to trades or portfolio expenses. Trade fees enter retained/disposed basis once; portfolio expenses enter attribution once; cash postings already contain those costs. Actual positive distributions and negative withholding are labelled gross/withholding/net. Embedded fund expenses and current fee tariffs are never synthesized as historical cash costs.

Required trusted settings are explicit evidence objects, rather than product-name inference:

- `source_timezone: {name, verified: true, evidence}` establishes actual broker local-time semantics. UTC conversion rejects DST ambiguity/nonexistent times; missing verification retains the legacy filter with unavailable diagnostics and withholds every cutoff-dependent held, realized and total gain. A genuinely empty input has no source timestamps; its verified cash-only opening baseline needs no source timezone.
- On 29 October 2026 the owner explicitly approved assuming `Europe/Amsterdam` without broker evidence. Configure `{name: "Europe/Amsterdam", verified: false, assumption_approved: true, evidence: "<dated owner decision>"}` to use this convention. This is the only accepted unverified timezone assumption; the approval marker must be boolean and evidence nonempty. Snapshot, Portfolio History and percentage results retain `source_timezone: {name, status: "owner_assumed", evidence}` and the Overview/Performance explain the assumption. Date-specific summer/winter rules apply; missing recorded times, ambiguous repeated times and nonexistent times remain rejected. The assumption enables timing-dependent calculations where all other inputs are covered, without asserting broker verification or complete price history.
- `asset_evidence[broker_asset_id]: {symbol, kind, verified: true, evidence}` establishes identity and quote mapping. Supported kinds are ETF, Stock, ETP and Cryptocurrency. A Bitcoin-named ETP never becomes spot crypto automatically.
- `opening_baseline: {at, cash_eur, equity_eur, verified: true, evidence}` must precede all Transactions. This first accounting contract supports a verified cash-only opening baseline (including verified zero before first funding). Existing opening holdings require reconciled opening lots and are rejected rather than invented.
- `cost_history: {verified: true, evidence}` attests completeness of the accepted fee/cost history. Without it, retained cost is explicitly `recorded_only_incomplete`; held/realized/total gains are unavailable, while independently observed market values remain available. An absent fee row is never proof of a zero fee.
- `fx_evidence[transaction_id]: {date, currency, eur_per_unit, provider, verified: true}` converts the posting at its actual ledger date. Unsupported foreign cash continues to reject publication through the existing audit boundary; unknown currency withholds dependent EUR amounts. The source `FX` field alone is not a verified conversion contract. Explicit Transactions AutoFX fee columns are actual costs, posted and allocated once.

Held and realized lot gains require complete dependent cost history and verified cutoff eligibility; independent known lot gains do not require a cash baseline. All-time net gain and its attributed total additionally require a verified opening baseline, and both totals remain unavailable together when that baseline is absent. Their attribution reconciles retained gain + realized gain + gross income - withholding - portfolio costs to end equity - opening equity - external contributions. A failed bridge or unavailable dependent cost withholds the total result. A missing trade cash posting also withholds dependent cash/equity; independently observed market value remains visible. A sale exceeding known inventory rejects the generation because short positions are outside this contract. Missing observed quotes never use legacy cost as market value. Missing cost never erases an independently observed value. Purchase/sale cash is internal portfolio activity, including sale proceeds reinvested in another Asset.

The calculation boundary does no I/O. Import identity reconciliation still belongs to the normalized import contract: feed accepted deduplicated Transactions, never concatenate raw overlapping exports. Tests exercise mixed Account/Transactions import overlap, exact source decimals, fee allocation, completed sales, the three mandatory worked examples, dated FX, timezone/DST and incomplete coverage. Private source tests run locally without creating a public financial fixture. Actual broker timezone, asset-kind evidence and opening/cost coverage remain unverified; the synthetic evidence is not production certification.
